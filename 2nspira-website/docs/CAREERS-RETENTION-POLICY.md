# Applicant record retention and accommodation handling

**Status: INTERIM PRESERVATION POLICY — final retention period NOT approved.**
Owner: 2Nspira hiring administration. Contact: hiring@2nspira.com.

## Scope

This policy covers application contact information, résumés/professional profiles, cover letters, questionnaire responses and question snapshots, hiring-status/decision history, legal-hold history, and recruitment email delivery records. It applies to the Careers database and private document storage. Any copies exported by authorized personnel remain subject to the same obligations; deleting an in-app record does not erase external mailbox copies or provider backups.

## While applicability is being determined

- Preserve all applicant and hiring records. No scheduled/automatic deletion runs.
- Retention remains `pending`, with no invented final number of days.
- The application deletion API refuses deletion while the policy is pending; the Admin control is disabled. Privacy erasure requests go to hiring@2nspira.com for review against recordkeeping and hold obligations, not immediate automatic erasure.
- Do not open real applications until the applicable recordkeeping requirements and the final notice have been approved and real email delivery/replies have been verified.

## Determining the final period

Confirm employer employee count, legal employer identity, relevant work locations (NY/NYC, NJ, CT), federal-contractor/subcontractor status and applicable contractual obligations. Document which requirements cover 2Nspira before choosing a period.

Federal EEOC guidance generally requires covered employers to preserve personnel/employment records for one year. Applicability and the triggering record/personnel-action date matter; this is **not** a declaration that a one-year policy is sufficient for 2Nspira. Separate ADEA/payroll/benefit rules, state obligations and any applicable contractor requirements must be assessed rather than conflated with applicant retention. A litigation limitation period is not automatically a record-retention rule.

Sources:
- [EEOC recordkeeping requirements](https://www.eeoc.gov/employers/recordkeeping-requirements)
- [29 CFR 1602.14: preservation of records and pending charges](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XIV/part-1602/subpart-C/section-1602.14)
- [EEOC summary of selected recordkeeping obligations](https://www.eeoc.gov/employers/summary-selected-recordkeeping-obligations-29-cfr-part-1602)

The final approval must identify the applicable rules, minimum period, triggering event and reviewer/approval basis in Admin. The implemented deletion guard measures the minimum from the later of creation and recorded hiring decisions, and preserves a longer period previously snapshotted for that applicant. An approved period does not enable automatic deletion: authorized personnel review records and holds first. The public notice describes this behavior, not an unimplemented purge schedule.

## Legal holds

A complaint, agency charge, preservation demand, threatened/pending litigation or another applicable hold overrides ordinary disposal. Preserve affected applicants and relevant comparator records; an EEOC charge can require records for others seeking similar positions through final disposition, including applicable appeals. The responsible hiring administrator must identify every affected record, place a hold in Admin with a non-sensitive reason, and retain associated external records as well.

Hold placement/release is recorded with administrator identity and time. A held application cannot be deleted. Release requires a documented reason after the responsible legal/recordkeeping reviewer authorizes release. Application, email-ledger and document deletion occurs in one conditional D1 batch; a concurrent hold or decision change prevents deletion of the stale snapshot.

## Private accommodation handling

Display beside the form: “For questions about your application or applicant privacy, or to request an accommodation during the application process, contact hiring@2nspira.com.”

Do not request medical/accommodation information in the role questionnaire. Requests go directly to the monitored hiring mailbox, not into ordinary candidate evaluation records. Authorized accommodation coordinators should keep supporting information separately with need-to-know access, sharing only the arrangements necessary to provide assistance. Do not copy diagnoses or supporting medical documents into application answers, hiring decision history or hold reasons. The website does not ingest mailbox messages into the applicant record.

Website enforcement is operational; mailbox delivery, mailbox access restrictions, coordinator assignment and separate mailbox record handling require independent verification. Do not describe these external controls as verified merely because the website displays a mailto link.

## Published notice

The form explains the contact fields, documents and questionnaire information collected, recruitment use, limited hiring-personnel access and necessary service-provider processing. Pending and approved retention wording are separate. Records subject to legal holds are preserved. Do not publish a fixed-period promise while the policy is pending, and do not promise erasure in conflict with a legal recordkeeping duty.
