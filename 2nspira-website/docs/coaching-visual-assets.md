Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium editorial hero illustration for the 2Nspira Entrepreneur Mentoring & Coaching website. Create a powerful, restrained modern image about turning a venture idea into a focused path forward while growing as a leader. A sculptural deep-navy folded ribbon/path rises from a set of open ivory architectural frames, connected by a single warm golden-yellow line of light, suggesting purposeful progress and guidance, not a generic staircase-to-success. Refined tactile paper and matte materials, soft natural directional light, crisp contemporary composition, off-white background, navy and muted blue, small yellow accents. Wide landscape image, focal composition centered with generous breathing room and safe crop on mobile. Sophisticated human warmth through material and light, not corporate clip art. No people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no graphs, no trophy, no money, no claims of guaranteed success. The image is conceptual, not a portrait or documentary photograph.

The page uses existing scoped catalog progressive effects: one-time section entrances and restrained hover/focus feedback. Native HTML details/summary preserve access to the full text without JavaScript. Reduced-motion preferences disable motion. No looping animation, parallax, scroll capture or new dependencies.
